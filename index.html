Questions ranchers ask

What is hanging weight?

It's the weight of the carcass hanging in the processor's cooler, after slaughter and before it's cut and wrapped. Most ranchers who sell by the whole, half or quarter charge by the pound of hanging weight.

Your buyer takes home less than that, roughly 60–65%, because bone, fat and trim come off. The calculator shows both, so you can explain it to your buyers.

Do I need a computer?

No. The calculator works on your phone. The call with Allen can be a regular phone call, or a video call on your phone if you'd like to look at your numbers together. No computer needed.

What happens after I book the $197 call?

You pick phone or video and up to 3 times that suit you, then pay through Stripe. Allen confirms your time by email within 24 hours. Before the call, jot down your rough numbers: what a calf is worth, feed and hay, processing, hauling and how many head.

What if I don't sell?

We promise the work, not a sales number. On Ranch Store in a Box, everything in the box gets done on schedule: the store, the listings, the flyer, the outreach, the posts, the reminders and the reports. Nobody can honestly promise how many shares you'll sell. Your day 30, 60 and 90 reports show you exactly what went out and what came back, so you and Allen can adjust.

Can I cancel or reschedule the call?

Yes. Cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled call for a full refund. You can reschedule once at no cost with 24 hours' notice. Calls missed without notice are not refunded.

Is my information shared?

No. We use your name, email and ranch information only to deliver your call and follow up about it. We do not sell or share your information.
