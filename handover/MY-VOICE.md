# Teach your agents to sound like you

Your Outreach agent (and later Follow-Up and Upgrade) writes in your voice. It learns from two things:

1. **A "My voice" email.** One email you send yourself that describes how you talk. Make it once with the prompt below.
2. **The emails you actually send.** Every time you edit one of its drafts and press Send, it learns from your sent version. Edit freely.

## Make your "My voice" email (about 10 minutes, once)

Open your Hanging Weight Co. project in Claude, start a new chat, and paste this:

```
Interview me so my email helpers can write exactly like I talk. Ask me one question at a time, short and plain, and wait for my answer. Cover: how I say hello and goodbye, how I'd describe my free calculator to another rancher, words and sayings I use a lot, words I'd never use, how formal or casual I am, how long my emails usually are, anything about my ranch and background I'm happy to mention, and anything I never want said. Then read the last 15 emails I wrote myself in my Gmail Sent folder to check your notes against how I really write.

When we're done, write my voice guide: under 300 words, plain bullet points, plus 2 short example invitations to a rancher written the way I'd write them. Then create a Gmail draft to calfroperblue@gmail.com with the subject "My voice – Hanging Weight Co." and the guide as the body. I'll read it, fix anything, and press Send myself.
```

Read it over, change anything that doesn't sound like you, and press **Send**. Your agents look for the newest email with that subject, so to update it later, just send a new one with the same subject.
