## Start with these 6

Turn these on first: **Rancher Finder, Outreach, Follow-Up, Call Prep, Website Watchdog and Daily Driver** (numbers 1 to 6 below). Add the others one at a time once you're comfortable.

Turn on the client helpers (15–19) when your first $2,000 client starts.

## How to turn one on

Do this once for each helper. Each one takes about 5 minutes. Start with Daily Driver and Website Watchdog.

1. Open Claude (claude.ai) on your computer and sign in.
2. Make sure your Gmail is connected: click your name or picture (bottom left), then **Settings**, then **Connectors**. Find **Gmail** and click **Connect**. Sign in with the Google account that gets your theherd@herddirect.net mail and your Stripe receipts, and click **Allow**. If theherd@herddirect.net isn't a Gmail or Google inbox, ask DJ to forward it into calfroperblue@gmail.com first. If you use Google Calendar for your calls, connect that too. You only do this once.
3. In the left sidebar, click **Scheduled tasks** (it may be called **Tasks**).
4. Click **New task**.
5. Open the helper's page (for example `daily-driver.md`). Copy everything below the line that says **"Copy everything below this line into the scheduled task"**, and paste it into the big box.
6. For **Outreach, Follow-Up, Partner Outreach, Upgrade and Local Buyer Finder** only: find the words **[Your mailing address]** and type your business mailing address in their place. You only do this once per helper. If a helper's page has a **Before you save it** part, do what it says too.
7. Give the task the helper's name (for example "Daily Driver").
8. Pick the schedule shown at the top of the helper's page under **When it runs** (for example, Daily at 7:30 AM). Check the time zone says Central.
9. If it asks what it can use, turn on **Gmail** and **Web search** (and **Google Calendar** for Booking, Call Prep and Daily Driver, if you connected it).
10. Click **Save**.

The buttons may be named a little differently or sit in a different spot by the time you read this. If you get stuck, call DJ.

## Your daily 10 minutes

1. **Read the morning email** ("Good morning – your day at Hanging Weight Co."). It tells you the 3 things to do.
2. **Open Gmail and click the label "Ready to send"** in the left column. If it isn't there, look in **Drafts**.
3. **Read each email.** If one starts with a line that says **"ALLEN:"**, do what that line says and delete the line. Change anything you like. Press **Send** on the ones you're happy with. Delete any you don't like.
4. **Before each $197 call, read the "Call prep" email** for that rancher.
5. **After each $197 call, email yourself your notes** with the subject **Call notes – <rancher's name>**. Call Recap turns them into his written plan.
6. If you get an email starting **"Website alert"**, do what it says. If it says urgent, call DJ that day.

Once a week you'll also get an article draft, three post drafts for your Facebook page, and your Monday numbers. Use what you like.

## Rules your helpers follow

- **They never send an email to anyone but you.** They only write drafts for ranchers, buyers and businesses. The only emails they send on their own are the ones to you.
- **They never make things up.** No made-up ranches, people, numbers, stories, reviews or promises. If they don't know something, they say "ask on the call", "not found", or leave a blank for you to fill in.
- **They never use Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok or any social media.** They only use public business listings, ranch and business websites, and public sources like university extension and USDA pages. Post Writer writes for your Facebook page, but you do the posting; it never looks at Facebook.
- **They only collect business contact details** that a ranch or business publishes itself, never personal ones.
- **Anyone who says "no thanks" never hears from you again.** Every invitation says so.
- **They write like you:** plain, friendly, rancher to rancher. No price promises, no claims about results, no health or grass-fed claims.
- **They never mention computers or software** in anything that goes to a rancher or a buyer. Every email is simply from you.
- **If there's nothing to do, they stay quiet.** The only emails you'll always get are the morning email, the weekly numbers, the article and post drafts, and client reports on day 30, 60 and 90.
- **They only look at your websites.** They never log in or change anything.
