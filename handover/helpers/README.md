# Your 6 helpers

Allen, these six helpers do the desk work for Hanging Weight Co. while you're out on the place. They live in your own Claude account and read your own Gmail. They never send anything to a rancher without you. They write the email, and you decide whether to press Send.

| Helper | What it does for you | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Rancher Finder** | Finds 15–20 new Texas ranches that sell beef direct, from public business listings, and emails you the list. | Every morning, 5:10 |
| **Outreach** | Writes up to 10 short, friendly invitations from you to those ranches, pointing them to your free calculator. | Weekday mornings, 6:20 |
| **Follow-Up** | Writes the thank-you and follow-up emails for people who used the calculator, and a "you're booked" email for $197 calls. | Every morning, 6:40 |
| **Call Prep** | Emails you a one-page cheat sheet before each $197 call: who they are, their numbers, 3 questions, when to mention the $2,000 package. | Every morning, 7:00 |
| **Website Watchdog** | Checks your websites. Emails you **only** if something breaks. | Every 6 hours |
| **Daily Driver** | One short morning email: what came in yesterday and the 3 things to do today. | Every morning, 7:30 |

All times are Central.

## How to turn one on

Do this once for each helper. Each one takes about 5 minutes. Start with Daily Driver and Website Watchdog.

1. Open Claude (claude.ai) on your computer and sign in.
2. Make sure your Gmail is connected: click your name or picture (bottom left), then **Settings**, then **Connectors**. Find **Gmail** and click **Connect**. Sign in with the Google account that gets your theherd@herddirect.net mail and your Stripe receipts, and click **Allow**. If theherd@herddirect.net isn't a Gmail or Google inbox, ask DJ to forward it into calfroperblue@gmail.com first. If you use Google Calendar for your calls, connect that too. You only do this once.
3. In the left sidebar, click **Scheduled tasks** (it may be called **Tasks**).
4. Click **New task**.
5. Open the helper's page (for example `daily-driver.md`). Copy everything below the line that says **"Copy everything below this line into the scheduled task"**, and paste it into the big box.
6. For **Outreach** and **Follow-Up** only: find the words **[Your mailing address]** and type your business mailing address in their place. You only do this once per helper.
7. Give the task the helper's name (for example "Daily Driver").
8. Pick the schedule shown at the top of the helper's page under **When it runs** (for example, Daily at 7:30 AM). Check the time zone says Central.
9. If it asks what it can use, turn on **Gmail** and **Web search**.
10. Click **Save**.

The buttons may be named a little differently or sit in a different spot by the time you read this. If you get stuck, call DJ.

## Your daily 10 minutes

1. **Read the morning email** ("Good morning – your day at Hanging Weight Co."). It tells you the 3 things to do.
2. **Open Gmail and click the label "Ready to send"** in the left column. If it isn't there, look in **Drafts**.
3. **Read each email.** Change anything you like. Press **Send** on the ones you're happy with. Delete any you don't like.
4. **Before each $197 call, read the "Call prep" email** for that rancher.
5. If you get an email starting **"Website alert"**, do what it says. If it says urgent, call DJ that day.

## Rules your helpers follow

- **They never send an email to a rancher without you.** They only write drafts. The only emails they send on their own are the ones to you.
- **They never make things up.** No made-up ranches, numbers, stories or promises. If they don't know something, they say "ask on the call" or leave it blank.
- **They never use Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok or any social media.** They only use public business listings and ranch websites.
- **They only collect business contact details** that a ranch publishes itself, never personal ones.
- **Anyone who says "no thanks" never hears from you again.** Every invitation says so.
- **They write like you:** plain, friendly, rancher to rancher. No price promises, no claims about results.
- **They only look at your websites.** They never log in or change anything.
